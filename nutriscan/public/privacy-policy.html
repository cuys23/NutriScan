Privacy Policy

NutriSnap · Last updated: August 5, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account using Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, or email. We also collect images you upload for nutrition analysis to provide our core AI-powered services. Authentication and cloud storage are managed through Firebase.

AI Processing (Groq)

For food image analysis and nutrition estimation, we utilize Groq Cloud AI services. When you upload an image, it is temporarily processed by Groq to generate nutrition data. We do not use your personal images to train general AI models.

Payments & Subscriptions

We use Google Play Billing and Apple App Store In-App Purchases to handle premium subscriptions. All payment information is processed securely by Google or Apple; we do not store your credit card details on our own servers.

Advertising (AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain data (like advertising identifiers) to show personalized ads. Premium users can enjoy an ad-free experience.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. This includes device information, app performance data, and general usage patterns — it does not include the contents of your food photos or nutrition logs.

Data Security & Storage

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Firestore and Firebase Auth. We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures provided by Google Cloud Platform to protect your information against unauthorized access.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the Settings menu in the app at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your cloud-stored data, photos, and account record.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@nutriscan.ai. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.
